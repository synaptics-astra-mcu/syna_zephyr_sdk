Synaptics Astra Series - Zephyr

The Synaptics Astra™ family of processors represents a new generation of intelligent, energy-efficient edge computing solutions designed to power AI-driven devices across industrial, consumer, and IoT markets. The Astra portfolio is broadly categorized into two series — SL (MPU) and SR (MCU) — each tailored to meet specific performance, power, and integration requirements.

The Astra SL series (MPU) is designed for applications requiring higher compute capability, rich operating system support, and advanced multimedia processing. They deliver powerful AI acceleration, high-performance application processing, and extensive connectivity, making them ideal for smart displays, edge gateways, industrial HMI, robotics, and vision-enabled systems. The SL line combines scalable performance with energy-efficient operation to enable sophisticated edge AI workloads.

The Astra SR series (MCU) focuses on ultra-low power consumption and real-time control, making it ideal for always-on sensing, smart home devices, wearables, battery-operated edge nodes, and embedded control systems. Solutions such as the SR100 integrate efficient AI acceleration, secure processing, and flexible connectivity options, enabling real-time inference and intelligent decision-making at the edge while maintaining extended battery life.

Across both SL and SR series, the Astra family emphasizes:

  - Integrated AI acceleration for real-time edge intelligence
  - Power-efficient architectures optimized for edge deployments
  - Rich connectivity and I/O support for flexible system integration
  - Robust security frameworks, including secure boot and hardware root of trust
  - Comprehensive software ecosystem support, enabling faster development and deployment of AI-enabled solutions

Together, the Synaptics Astra SL (MPU) and SR (MCU) processors provide a scalable platform that allows developers and OEMs to build intelligent, connected, and secure edge devices — ranging from low-power sensing and control nodes to high-performance AI and multimedia edge systems.
